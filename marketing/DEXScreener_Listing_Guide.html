Plain-language summaries of DEX Screener's listing pages (fetched July 31, 2026) + the claim plan for the Aladdin's Castle token

1 · How listings work (their docs)

  • Listing is automatic and free. A token appears on DEX Screener the moment it sits in a liquidity pool and has one transaction. $ACM is already listed — nothing to apply for.
  • Profile info is pulled, not submitted. Logo, description, and socials are sourced from external token lists — primarily CoinGecko. If a token is on CoinGecko, its info flows in automatically (the free-but-slow path: getting CoinGecko-listed first).
  • Enhanced Token Info is the skip-the-line product — pay and your info displays regardless of third-party listing status.
  • Market cap / FDV math: FDV = (total supply − burned) × price. With Enhanced Token Info you can designate locked-supply wallets so displayed market cap reflects reality — directly relevant to the 500M $ACM in Streamflow escrow.
  • Search works by name, symbol, contract, or pair address.
