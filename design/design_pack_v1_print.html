Locked doctrine

Four rules the whole design hangs on

MTC lives in our ledger

A row in our own database wearing a token's costume. Not crypto, no transfer verb, expires by clock.

The chain is read-only

One bridge: "does this wallet hold at least 1 $ACM?" Nothing is ever written on-chain by the app.

QR now, NFC later

Rotating one-time codes, generated on the phone with zero signal. Tap-to-redeem is Phase 2.

Network is optional

Connectivity is an optimization, never a dependency. Readers store and forward; coupons work offline.

A Meme Token Credit (MTC) is a non-transferable, time-limited promotional credit issued by the company and recorded on its private ledger. An MTC is not a cryptocurrency, not a blockchain token, and has no cash value. It exists only within the app and expires if unused.

Deliverable 3

The redemption handshake

The moment at the counter, including the dead-zone case. The burn is one atomic write, so a code can never be spent twice.

sequenceDiagram
  participant P as Phone (works offline)
  participant K as Reader kiosk
  participant L as MTC ledger
  P->>K: rotating code, generated on the phone
  K->>L: burn request
  L-->>K: burned exactly once
  K-->>K: big green REDEEMED
  Note over K,L: offline? queue and sync later
