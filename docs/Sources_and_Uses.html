The ceiling, and it is a real one

Ten million released plus 77.8 million bought is 87.8 million members at one whole $ACM each. Beyond that, tokens must come from holders choosing to sell.

The 99¢ never fails on economics — at 100 million downloads the token still costs about a cent against 69 cents of contribution. What ends the program is running out of tokens to give, not running out of money to buy them.

Instagram drew 762,300,000 downloads globally in 2025; this program supports 87.8 million of that, and the ceiling would be visible years in advance.
