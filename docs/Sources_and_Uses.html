06

The decision that actually matters

$ACM carries six decimals. The grant does not have to be a whole token, and eligibility is a threshold test — the system asks whether a holder has a credential, not how large it is. That makes grant size a free parameter, and it is the only thing that moves the ceiling.

At 0.01 ACM per download the released ten-million pool alone covers a billion downloads and no purchase is ever required. At 1 ACM it covers ten million and the ceiling is 101.6 million however much you spend.

Two consequences to weigh, not resolved here. A fractional grant is harder to describe than "you own one $ACM," and it removes even the small float-reduction effect a whole-token grant produces. Both are messaging and tokenomics questions, not engineering ones. If the whole-token grant is kept for narrative reasons, the ceiling of 101.6M downloads is a deliberate, priced choice — not an oversight.
