### The house rules

- Every contract is fully collateralized at signing. Both sides. No margin, no
  leverage, no default risk.
- Everything is denominated and paid in SOL — never in the thinly traded coin.
- Every escrow is a dedicated on-chain wallet you can watch yourself. We
  publish the receipts.
- All sales final. We're a market, not a promise.
