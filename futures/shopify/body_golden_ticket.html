WHAT THE GOLDEN TICKET IS

The Golden Ticket is a Meme Token Credit (MTC) — an entry on Instar's private, off-chain ledger that records you as one of the Top 100 at the freeze instant. It is a certificate of standing in the ACM relaunch, not a coin, not a security, and not something for sale. What it will be redeemable for is announced with the relaunch; the certificate itself is the permanent record that you were there.

HOW THE FREEZE WORKS

  • The instant: 12:00:00 PM Eastern (16:00:00 UTC), Mon Sept 7, 2026. Our snapshot records the exact Solana slot, so anyone can re-derive the same list from the chain.
  • Who counts: every wallet holding $ACM, aggregated by owner (all your token accounts count together), ranked by balance. Non-human accounts — the two supply-lock escrows, the liquidity pool, house wallets, and contract escrow — are excluded and listed separately above.
  • Certification: each of the 100 rows becomes a leaf (sha256(rank|wallet|balance)); the leaves build a Merkle tree; the root is published here, on the ACM page, and in the ACM public records. Every holder gets their proof.
  • Simple by design: there is no anti-gaming rule. What the chain says at that slot is the list. Can't un-game crypto.

AFTER THE FREEZE

This page becomes the sealed table with the Merkle root at the top and a downloadable certificate (JSON) containing every leaf and proof. Golden Ticket MTCs are issued to the 100 wallets from the sealed list. Not investment advice; the Golden Ticket confers no ownership, revenue, or governance rights.
